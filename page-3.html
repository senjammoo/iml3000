Distance

All of this for a nerf gun?
Or so I thought…
Me and my friends were given two options…

[ Accept the Violation ] 4 years of academic probation.
A weapons charge.
A flag on our records.

[ Or Appeal ] Still having probation, the charge,
and the flag on record until proven innocent.
Possibly worse punishment and even expulsion.
And a court hearing.
My friends accepted,
while I chose to dispute.
What followed was a long and drawn out,
stressful process.

[ What that looked like ] Meetings with school faculty.
Meetings with my student support group.
Managing classes, sports, and social life…
I felt confused,
frustrated,
sad,
and drained…
