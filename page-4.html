Light

I found my light, my polaris, through community… I was fortunate enough to have good people around me, a student support group, who cared for me and understood my innocence.
With this group, we discovered that the violation had nothing to do with me or my friends. The initial report stated we drove around in a car, playing loud music, wearing ski masks, shooting at random people with bb-guns… which was not true. We found that it was actually a student from a neighboring school who had campus safety called on him. The school pinned it on me and my friends.
With this information, we felt confident that I would be relieved of my violations.
