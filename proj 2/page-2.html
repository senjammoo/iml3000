Darkness

In my freshman year of college,
I played football.
We started the season off 0 – 5.
Zero wins.
Around this time, the town I was in
experienced a mass shooting.
Overall morale was low.
So, me and my friends bought nerf guns
from the local Walmart,
starting a game of assassin on campus.
[ Game Rules ] You hit someone with a nerf gun, you get a point.
NO playing in classrooms or school buildings.
Be safe, have fun.

It was fun…
Until my friends were notified of a
student conduct violation,
threatening suspension, expulsion,
a weapons charge,
and a flag raised on record.

I vouched for my friends' innocence —
and I too received the same notice…
